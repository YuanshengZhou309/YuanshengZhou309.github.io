## Background

This research examined neural responses to five basic tastes and a tasteless control using scalp electroencephalography (EEG).

## My work and methods

I processed and analysed EEG responses using feature selection and spectral and temporal comparisons across brain regions. I contributed to the study's conceptualization and formal analysis.

## Outcome

The results were published in *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control* in 2026. See the [publication and DOI]({{ '/publications/taste-eeg/' | relative_url }}) for the citation.
