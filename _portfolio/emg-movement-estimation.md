## Background

Estimating movement intention from muscle activity has applications in assistive robotics. This project considered both hand-action classification and joint-angle estimation from electromyography (EMG).

## My work and methods

I processed NinaPro EMG signals, extracted features, and developed machine-learning models for classification and regression. The work applied time-series and biomedical-signal processing to movement-intention estimation.

## Outcome

The project developed models for the two tasks: recognising hand actions and estimating joint angles from EMG features.
