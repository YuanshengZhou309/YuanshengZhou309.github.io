## Background

This project addressed automated visual inspection and sorting of pharmaceutical cartridges in a GMP-oriented setting.

## My work and methods

I integrated a UR3e robot with two SICK 3D cameras. My work included robot control, safety configuration, HMI functionality, testing, and documentation in Siemens TIA Portal.

## Outcome

The project brought robot control and 3D vision together for cartridge inspection and sorting, with the associated HMI, testing, and documentation.
