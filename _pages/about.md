I am Yuansheng Zhou, an MSc graduate in Autonomous Systems from the Technical University of Denmark (DTU), based in Kongens Lyngby, Denmark. My work connects machine learning and signal processing with sensing and automated systems.

My research and project experience spans binaural acoustic authentication, EEG and EMG analysis, medical image generation, and pharmaceutical automation. I have worked on experimental pipelines from sensor data acquisition and preprocessing through model evaluation, as well as robot and vision integration.

## Research interests

- **Machine Learning:** classification, regression, and generative models for sensor and image data.
- **Signal Processing:** time-frequency representations, feature extraction, and acoustic and biomedical signal analysis.
- **Intelligent Sensing:** binaural sensing, information across sensing channels, and robustness in real-world measurements.
- **Robotics and Automation:** movement-intention estimation, robot control, and vision-based inspection.
